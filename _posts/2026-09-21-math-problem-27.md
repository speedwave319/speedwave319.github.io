### Question

(a) Prove that $\displaystyle \int_{1}^{2} x^a(\ln x)^2\,dx = \frac{2^{a+1}(\ln2)^2}{a+1} -\frac{2^{a+2}(\ln2)}{(a+1)^2}+\frac{2^{a+2}-2}{(a+1)^3}$ for any constant $a \geq 0$.

(b) Using (a) and integration by substitution, evaluate $\displaystyle \int_{1}^{2} x \big[ \ln (\sqrt x+1) \big]^2\,dx$. 

(c) Evaluate $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin x \left[ \ln \left(\sqrt {\sin \frac{x}{2} }+1\right) \right]^2\,dx$. 

(d) Evaluate $\displaystyle \int_{3\pi}^{4\pi} \sin x \left[ \ln \left(\sqrt {\cos \frac{x}{2} }+1\right) \right]^2\,dx$.
