### Question

A random sample of 11 students was drawn from a school and the weight of each student was measured. The sa mple mean and sample standard deviation of these weights were then calculated to be 48.90 kg and 15.75 kg respectively. Upon checking the data, it was found that the weight of Mario, one of the students, was wrongly recorded. After making necessary correction, the sample mean and sample standard deviation were found to be 49.08 kg and 15.68 kg respectively.

Find the actual weight of Mario. Answer should be given to the nearest 0.01 kg .


(5 marks)
